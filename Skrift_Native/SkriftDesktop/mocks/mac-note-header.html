The Mac note header, at the iPad's weight

Comparing the two apps side by side, the Mac's note header is roughly twice the vertical weight of the iPad's for the same information — a title chooser plus a four-row properties table before you reach a word of the note. The iPad gets there with one chips row and an importance row. This proposes the same for the Mac, and says where each displaced field goes. Tokens and copy below are read from the current source, not redrawn from memory.

So I was talking with [[Nick Jansen]] this morning about collapsing the whole thing into one native app.

1Title is one editable line; the suggested-vs-recording choice becomes two quiet words under it. Same decision, a tenth of the space.
2source and duration become chips (outlined above) — the iPad already chips source for captures and video.
3Importance keeps its circles but loses the card chrome — same block the iPad shows.
4Tags join the one chips row instead of living in a separate compartment.
5Include audio keeps a real switch, at half size, under importance — a per-note decision you flip while reading.

Where the displaced fields go

Nothing is deleted. Each field moves to where it's actually used — the test being whether you'd look for it while reading a note.

FieldGoes toWhy
author Out of the header — shown as a chip only when it differs from your Settings author It's you on every note. It still goes into the exported frontmatter, which is the only place it's read.
source A chip, with the same taxonomy glyph as the sidebar row One word. The iPad already does exactly this for captures and video imports.
duration A chip Five characters. The docked player also shows it as the scrubber's end time.
Include audio in export Stays a toggle in the header — just small, under importance Tuur, 2026-07-25: it's a per-note decision you flip while looking at the note, so it keeps a real switch rather than hiding behind a menu. Small is enough.
url · reminder · locked Chips (they're already conditional rows today) Reminder is already a chip on the iPad; a lock chip reads faster than a sentence in a table.
date Stays a chip (it already is one) Also still in the breadcrumb above the band — that repetition is worth a look during sign-off.

Include audio on the iPad — what it actually costs

Tuur asked for this toggle on the iPad too. It belongs there, but it is not the same size of job as the rest of this page, and the reason is worth knowing before it's scheduled.

FactConsequence
The flag is Mac-only today includeAudioInExport lives on PipelineFile (the Mac's own model), is read only by VaultExporter, and does not exist on Memo — so it never syncs.
The phone's publisher copies no audio at all ObsidianPublisher writes markdown only. So the toggle can't govern the iPad's own publish — there's nothing there for it to govern.
Therefore, on the iPad it means "when the Mac exports this, bring the audio" Which is coherent — the iPad is the review station, the Mac obeys — but it makes this a synced field, not a UI addition: a Memo property, CloudKit ingest + update + write-back, and the Mac's copy driven by the synced value under last-write-wins.
The sync contract is the spine Per CLAUDE.md that's a deliberate change with its own verification, so it should be its own chunk rather than riding along with a header redesign.

Recommendation: build the header now (Mac + the iPad's matching row), and land the synced flag as the next chunk — the iPad's switch stays hidden until the field it writes actually reaches the Mac, so it can never lie about what will happen at export.

The one judgement call

The title chooser is the only Mac-only affordance being compressed — it's where you decide whether a note is titled by the model or by its recording. If two quiet words feel too weak for a decision you make on every note, the alternative is keeping the two cards but halving their height. Everything else here is a straight port of what the iPad already does.
